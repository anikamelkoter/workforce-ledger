How the numbers work

Employee headcount

Distinct active employees on the reporting date. Contractors are excluded. Hire dates are inclusive; termination dates are the first day no longer employed.

Historical organization

SQL resolves the department and region effective on the reporting date. Transfers change local counts without creating company-wide hires.

Monthly turnover

Employee departures divided by average opening and closing employee headcount, multiplied by 100. Not annualized. Opening headcount is the previous month’s closing count.

Provisional reporting

Invalid employment records are set aside. Missing organizational fields appear as Unknown without removing valid people from global counts. The clean answer key is used only in tests.

About this demo

Python validates simulated exports, SQLite produces reports, and this page embeds the resulting snapshot. No real employee data, authentication, regional access enforcement, or live SAP connection is included. Filters control the display, not access permissions. Power BI integration remains a separate implementation step.
